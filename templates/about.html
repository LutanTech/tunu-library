Our Story

Books, community and straightforward access.

Tunu Library is an open platform for readers, researchers and students. We built this site to keep borrowing simple and make literature accessible to anyone with an internet connection.

Our collection covers classic fiction, regional releases and modern textbooks. Find the title you need, place a request and pick up your copy.

"A library exists to keep knowledge within reach for anyone willing to open a book."

— Lutan

How We Operate

We focus on system efficiency and minimal user friction. The app syncs inventory in real time, processes payments via M-Pesa and works offline when network coverage drops.
